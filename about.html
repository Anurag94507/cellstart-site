⚡ Our Origin & Vision

Born to Fix Broken Charging

We started CellStart with a simple frustration: frayed rubber cables, slow charging bricks, and wasteful throwaway tech.

🌱

100% Recyclable Packaging

We eliminated all single-use plastics from our retail boxes, using FSC-certified unbleached kraft paper and soy-based non-toxic inks.

🛡️

Engineered for 5+ Years

The greenest charging cable is the one you never need to replace. By building with Kevlar weave and GaN semiconductors, we keep e-waste out of landfills.

⚡

Open PD Standards

We believe in universal USB-IF Power Delivery standards so one single CellStart charger powers all your hardware seamlessly.
